Why it exists

One source of truth is how money gets lost.

Vetting a counterparty with a single scan, dashboard, or gut-feel leaves a hole — and that's where the honeypot slips through. SentinelVault closes it by asking many independent on-chain + intelligence signals at once.

⊞

Never rely on one scan

URL safety, fraud pattern, CVE exposure, tx status, holder concentration, TVL and sentiment — checked together, not one tool in isolation.

⚖

One clear decision

Approve, Flag, or Block with a 0–100 risk score and a confidence % — so you know exactly how sure to be, and whether to escalate.

✓

Proof, not promises

Every signal carries its on-chain settlement hash. What a miner told you is auditable — no screenshots, no "trust me".

For developers

One call, in any language.

SentinelVault is a plain HTTP API plus an IDL-free SDK. Point your app at it and gate your own integrations on the verdict.

import { SentinelVault } from '@sentinelvault/sdk'

const sv = new SentinelVault({ baseUrl, apiKey })

const v = await sv.screen('0x28C6…d60', 'token')
if (v.verdict === 'BLOCK') rejectDeal(v.proof)
